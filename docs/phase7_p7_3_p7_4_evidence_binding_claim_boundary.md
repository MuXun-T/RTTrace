# P7.3/P7.4 Evidence Binding Claim Boundary

This corrective freeze may claim that four named simulator-generated FreeRTOS
raw traces have real per-case P7.3 manifests, deterministic P7.3 validator
reopens, and machine-readable P7.2 raw trace to P7.3 validated artifact to
P7.4 replay-report identity bindings.  The bindings and per-case reports are
repeatable canonical artifacts.  Existing P7.3 package-open and P7.4 replay
semantics are unchanged.

Report-only facts are package, artifact, binding, and report counts; byte
sizes; SHA-256 values; and observed runtime.  `hardware_validation=false`;
`proof_parity_eligible=false`; `proof_parity=not_evaluated`;
`proof_correctness=not_evaluated`; and `diagnosis_correctness=not_evaluated`.

This freeze does not claim proof parity or correctness, diagnosis/root-cause
correctness, generic FreeRTOS/RTOS support, real hardware validation,
performance, baseline superiority, SOTA, or any P7.5/P7.6 outcome.  P7.5 is
not started and P7.6 is not started.  Zephyr and Zephelin are excluded: no raw
trace, P7.3 case package, artifact closure, or trace-package binding is
asserted for either.
