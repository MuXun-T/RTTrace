# P7.7 Claim Boundary

P7.7 may preserve independent baseline inventory, declared capability,
comparability gates, exclusion reasons, input/source/version/license evidence,
and raw result records including failed runs. It may report a limited matched
performance comparison only when all seven gates pass: same raw-trace SHA-256,
case/workload, input scope, independently matched truth boundary, identical or
approved-equivalent environment, metric/unit/statistics, and failure/exclusion
policy. A quantitative result retains raw samples, failures, exclusions, sample
count, median, MAD, minimum, maximum, and environment identity.

Current P7.7 has zero quantitative baselines. Its six candidate categories are
unverified `not_evaluated`, capability-only inventory records. No candidate has
claimed support, availability, installation, license, input compatibility, or
comparability. No tool was downloaded, installed, probed, or executed.

P7.7 does not claim diagnosis accuracy, root-cause accuracy, replay
correctness, proof correctness, proof parity, general RTOS advantage,
anomaly-detection SOTA, overall tool superiority, human usability, remediation
safety, or publication acceptance likelihood. It does not rank a baseline on
correctness-like properties. An unsupported capability is `not_applicable`, not
a failure; missing evidence is `not_evaluated` or `not_comparable`, never zero.

P7.7 neither changes nor reinterprets Phase 6 or P7.1--P7.6 frozen artifacts,
hashes, expected outputs, semantics, or claims. P7.8 has not begun.
