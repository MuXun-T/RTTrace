# P7.5 Claim Boundary

P7.5 may claim deterministic, offline, layered validation of the six named
P7.4 replay-report cases against their frozen P7.2, P7.3, P7.4, binding,
expected-fixture, and comparison-profile evidence. It may claim canonical
P7.5 reports only when the frozen replay result is reproduced, equivalent, and
free of replay-fact drift.

The four acquired FreeRTOS cases are report-only validation results. Zephyr
and Zephelin remain `reference_only`; P7.5 does not create or infer missing
trace, package, replay, expected-output, or proof evidence for either case.
`hardware_validation=false`. Production validation truth paths record
`LLM=0`, `Advisor=0`, `Feedback=0`, `Network=0`, `Shell=0`, and
`Subprocess=0`.

The frozen proof comparison domain is empty. Therefore
`proof_parity_eligible=false`, `proof_parity=not_evaluated`,
`proof_correctness=not_evaluated`, and `diagnosis_correctness=not_evaluated`.
P7.5 does not claim proof parity or correctness, diagnosis or root-cause
correctness, hardware validation, general RTOS support, performance,
scalability, human usability, automatic remediation safety, or P7.6 work.
P7.6 is not started.
