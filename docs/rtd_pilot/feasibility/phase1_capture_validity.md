# Phase 1 Capture validity rules (frozen before capture)

A Capture is invalid if it uses a pin map other than `phase1-pinmap-v2`, has a board ID other than `fire-f103zet6-v2-1`, or has a target/probe binding other than `stm32f103ze`/`0001A0000001`; has an artifact/hash/channel/profile mismatch; uses a rate below its frozen DSView profile minimum; has nonflat channels inconsistent with its firmware variant; target reset/halt; firmware/ELF/build/board/session mismatch; observer loss/truncation; unreported platform/wiring/load change; or drift/alignment beyond bounds frozen from the first accepted real calibration series.

`BASE` and `GPIO_ONLY` are perturbation baselines: they require complete raw/normalized observer evidence and a matching variant channel-activity summary, but must not be rejected merely because they intentionally emit no UART epoch or clock-analysis artifact. Variants with UART require nonempty UART and clock-analysis artifacts, same-id contiguous BEGIN/END pairs, and matching Observer epoch sequences. `calibration_20mhz` requires at least 20 MHz; `IRQ_SMOKE` and `COMBINED_SMOKE` require `irq_precision_100mhz` at at least 100 MHz because PB5 is a short ISR marker.

The validator at `hardware/rtd_pilot/scripts/validate_capture.py` enforces metadata and file/hash/channel/epoch/sequence/reset checks fail-closed. It accepts an optional already-frozen drift maximum. It does not examine parser or diagnoser output. Controlled corrupt copies are validator tests only, never hardware evidence. Injection enabled with no manifest is retained as `non-manifested`, not automatically technically invalid. Invalid and non-manifested captures are retained.
